# Give the engine a credit

If you make and share a piece with Living Sometimes Engine, please credit it in both places:

- **Within the piece:** include the name **Living Sometimes Engine**, a symbol, or something else that represents it. Make it part of the work in whatever way suits your story. Placement, timing and visual treatment are yours.
- **In the announcement post:** credit **Living Sometimes Engine** by name. Please link to [the engine repository](https://github.com/Tally-DE/living-sometimes-engine) where possible so other people can use it too.

For example, an announcement can say: “Made with Living Sometimes Engine.” Your wording is up to you.

There is no prescribed watermark, logo, credit screen or size. For agents, carry this request into the story brief and release notes; leave the artistic treatment to the author. The engine does not insert a credit automatically.

**These creative and announcement credits are entirely optional. They are appreciated, never required.** This is a community request, not an additional licence condition. The engine remains under the unmodified [MIT licence](LICENSE), including its requirement to preserve the copyright and permission notice in copies or substantial portions of the software. Your work and assets remain yours.

Pieces made here are live rendered code. This request does not introduce recording or video export.
